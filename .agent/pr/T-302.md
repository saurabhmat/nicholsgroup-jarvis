# T-302: Finance cash report (book balance per bank account by week)

Third of the controller's reports (spec `docs/specs/finance.md` section 15.4), on top of T-300 and T-301. A "Cash" tab, second in the row (Overview,
Cash, Receivables, Invoices & payments), at `/[laneId]/finance/cash`: today's book balance of each bank account and its book balance at the end
(Friday, New York) of each of the last N completed business weeks (N = `lane_config.finance.reports.weeks`), the change from the next older column, a
total row, and an "as of" per column. Every figure is computed in SQL. "Book balance" everywhere (rule 7). No money in and out.

## What changed
- **SQL** `supabase/core/supabase/migrations/20261208100000_finance_cash_report.sql` (edited in place after review; unmerged)
  - `fin.cash_book_balances` (lane_id, connection, `fin.accounts` row, date, `book_balance numeric(18,2)`, `as_of`; unique per connection, account,
    date; standard finance-view RLS at AAL2; no write for authenticated).
  - `core.fin_cash_accounts` / `core.fin_cash_account_ids` (service_role): the cash accounts exactly as "Cash by account" lists them (bank, active, not
    deleted, mapped to the own entity).
  - `core.fin_cash_book_balances_put` (service_role): one date in one call; refuses the whole call for any account that is not one of those; an empty
    call clears the date; rows written must equal items.
  - `core.fin_cash_book_balances_purge` (service_role): retention (26 weeks), rows of disconnected connections, every row of the connection when the
    history switch is off. The job calls it first, on its own.
  - `core.fin_cash_history_enabled(lane)` (definer, one boolean, finance view at AAL2) for `finance.reports.cash_history` (default FALSE).
  - `core.business_week_start(date)` (immutable); `core.finance_cash(lane, weeks default null)` (invoker): Today column only while the switch is off.
- **Connector** `packages/connectors/src/quickbooks/cash-book-balances.ts` and `cash-book-balance-store.ts`; the reports job runs purge first and the
  history after the reports. Transient cash failures fail the run (retry); permanent ones are logged (`qbo.cash_history.not_stored`) and the run succeeds.
  **`balance_sheet` is not a cached report kind; nothing of a Balance Sheet is stored in `fin.report_cache`: D-27 is unchanged.**
- **Web** `apps/web/lib/finance-reports.ts`, `components/finance-cash.tsx`, `app/[laneId]/finance/cash/{page,loading,error}.tsx`.
- **Config** `finance.reports.cash_history` (boolean, default **false**) in `packages/core/src/lane-config/index.ts`; `packages/core/src/db/types.ts` regenerated.
- **Tests updated (not weakened):** tab tests, `050`, `335` (40+7=47), `336` (100 callable, 81 lane functions, 17 pure helpers), `339` (fin writers: put and
  purge), lane-config defaults, the reports test for default settings.
- Gitignored production SQL in `supabase/core/bootstrap/manual-migrations/`: `part-39-T-302.sql`, `part-39-T-302-check.sql` (21 rows),
  `pkg-cash-accounts-list.sql`, `enable-cash-history-T-302.sql`, `check-enable-cash-history-T-302.sql` (6 rows), `undo-enable-cash-history-T-302.sql`.

## Risks (for review)
1. **Payroll.** A bank account used only for payroll is flagged only by its NAME or by an id listed in `finance.payroll.account_ids`, not by type. That
   is why the history is OFF by default and a person looks at the accounts first (`pkg-cash-accounts-list.sql`).
2. **What of the Balance Sheet is kept.** Only `{ account id, balance }` of the lane's cash accounts; constant error messages; logs carry counts and dates.
3. **Own balance vs totals.** An account is kept only when it is on exactly one Data row and on no header/summary. Unverified against Intuit.
4. **Deletes.** The purge and the writer delete with WHERE; `service_role` has DELETE on this one table.
5. **Wording.** "Book balance"; no name in `fin`/`core` contains "bank balance" (pgTAP).
