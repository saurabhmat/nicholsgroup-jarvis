## Risks
- The Home figures are only as good as what the sync stores. See "Plan-review notes": with default settings **cash and P&L MTD will show a reason, not a number**, and AR > 30 is the only figure that works out of the box.
- `home_finance` is one function returning three figures; an exception in one would blank Home's finance row (it then shows "Could not load QuickBooks figures"). Plain SQL, no known failure.
- Realm (company) id is displayed in Settings to owner and admin_technical. It is an identifier, not a credential; the old test section avoided showing it, the spec for this task asks for it.

## Plan-review notes
Defaults taken (no human available), definitions chosen, and gaps:
1. **Cash needs the Balance Sheet in `fin.report_cache`, which is off by default (D-27: `finance.reports.kinds` defaults to pnl + aging only).** `fin.accounts` has no balance and `fin.transactions` has no opening balances, so a ledger sum would be wrong. Cash therefore shows "The balance sheet is not part of the QuickBooks sync yet" until D-27 allows `balance_sheet` to be cached. No estimate is made.
2. **The Balance Sheet request is class-filtered** by the connector when class tracking is on and a class list is configured (`REPORT_DEFS.balance_sheet.classFilter = true`). A class-filtered balance is not the account's book balance, so `home_finance` refuses it (`balance_sheet_class_filtered`). Follow-up for the reports job: fetch the Balance Sheet unfiltered (D-18: cash is shown in full under the owning entity, never allocated). I did not change the connector (out of scope).
3. **P&L month to date needs a report whose range starts on the 1st of the current business month.** The report job's only periods are `year_to_date` and `trailing_12_months`, so no MTD report exists and the card shows "The month-to-date report is not synced yet". Nothing is derived from YTD and nothing from ledger lines (invoice income is not in `fin.transactions`, and payroll is excluded by D-25). Follow-up: add a `month_to_date` period (needs `lane_config.finance.reports.period` change in `packages/core/src/lane-config/index.ts`, which I was told not to edit) or a second MTD pull in the reports job. The SQL already picks up such a report as soon as it is cached (params `start_date` = first of the ET month, `end_date` inside the month, newest `as_of` wins).
4. **AR > 30 definition**: sum of `fin.invoices.balance` where `entity_code` = `lane_config.entities.own_entity`, not voided/deleted, `balance > 0`, currency USD, and the due date (issue date when there is none) is **more than 30 days** before the America/New_York business date (a due date exactly 30 days ago is not counted; it falls in the 1-30 bucket). Invoices with entity `unmapped` that would qualify are counted separately and shown as a "not included" note, never allocated; invoices of other entities are excluded. Class-based entity split is whatever the normalizer already decided (`entity_code`), so this follows D-18 (class where available, else customer mapping).
5. **P&L MTD definition**: from the cached ProfitAndLoss: income = leaf (`Data`) rows of `Income` + `OtherIncome`; expenses = `COGS` + `Expenses` + `OtherExpenses`; net = income - expenses. Summary rows are ignored (the connector withholds totals when payroll rows were removed, D-25), so the figures are computed from kept rows. When totals were withheld the card says "Excludes payroll accounts" (`pnl_partial`): net is then **not** QuickBooks' net income. In `file_class` mode the report must be class-filtered, else `report_not_split_by_class`. Multi-column reports are refused.
6. **Shared bank account** (D-18): flagged when the account is listed under more than one owner in `lane_config.entities.bank_accounts`, or when its ledger entity (`fin.accounts.entity_code`) differs from the lane's own entity. A report bank account in no mapping is counted ("not mapped to a company and not included"), never summed. Accounts owned by another entity are left out.
7. **as_of** = last successful normalizer run (`fin.normalize_runs.finished_at`), else last sync end. Cash and P&L also show their report's own `as_of`. Freshness (stale threshold) is not shown: the SQL does not return `lane_config.freshness`, so the existing `SourceChip` (needs a freshness state) is not used.
8. A role without the finance view (admin_technical, outsider) sees no finance cards at all (previously the tiles said "Not available yet"). The calendar/inbox parts are untouched.
9. The "no_data" test is: connected, no normalizer run, no invoices and no cached reports. Connected with an existing but empty file shows "ok" with zero/empty reasons per figure.
10. `lane_config.pilot.connection_test_enabled` is now unused by code; the schema key and its test remain (dead config, safe to remove in a later cleanup together with its comment in `lane-config/index.ts`). Docs that mention the temporary section (`docs/runbooks/qbo-sandbox-run.md`, `docs/design/README.md`, `.agent/pr/T-198.md`, `.agent/pr/T-230.md`) were not edited (docs/backlog.md and plan-review.md are the lead's); the runbook's "Temporary: connection test" steps now map to Settings -> Connections -> QuickBooks.
