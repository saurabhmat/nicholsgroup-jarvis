## What changed
- **1. AI summary: automatic and reliable.**
  - **Structured output:** the briefing asks the API for structured output (`output_config.format`, JSON schema built the SDK's way: no `$schema`, `anyOf` instead of type arrays). The reply is checked with Zod, retried once with the issue paths and codes (never values), and failures are logged as codes only.
  - **Run now is gone.** A run starts in the background (`after()`) when the owner opens Home and the summary is older than 3 hours, and only if mail is connected.
  - **Never from auto-refresh:** the trigger is a client mount effect, guarded per tab, so the 5-minute refresh can't start it.
  - **SQL claims:** `brief.briefing_runs` plus `core.briefing_claim_*` enforce the 3-hour staleness, one run per lane at a time, and 6 runs per lane per New York day. Every claim counts toward the 6, lost and released ones too.
  - **Regenerate** sits in a ⋯ disclosure: owner only, once per 30 minutes.
  - **On the card:** "Updating summary…" shows while the previous summary stays visible, plus "Summary at <time>".
  - **Schedule:** the 06:30 Mon–Fri run (H-046) records a claim. A retry reuses a running claim, skips a day that already finished, and adds a counted claim after a failure.
- **2. Needs your attention.**
  - **"Today at a glance":** a cream panel with a gold edge holding the summary (up to 3 lines), Top priorities (3), and one muted line when AI is unavailable.
  - **Rows:**
    - Line 1: initials avatar, name, then the domain of the sender's ADDRESS (never from the display name; the full address is screen-reader text), and the age.
    - Line 2: the one-line subject.
    - Line 3: why → next.
    - At most 2 chips; Reply plus Open; the whole row opens the thread (stretched link).
  - **Mailbox:** hidden when the lane has one mailbox.
  - **Footer:** "View all in Inbox · N need attention →".
- **3. Calendar.**
  - A time column: local time bold, the event's own zone small below.
  - Attendee names, never emails, then "+N more".
  - At most 2 chips; a small "Join" button (https, shows its host); the title links to the event.
  - Next 7 days is collapsed with a summary line ("12 meetings, 2 clashes").
  - Duplicate invitations are deduped.
- **4. KPI strip:** tiles only for connected sources. With none connected, one slim banner (admins only) linking to Settings. No revenue tiles without finance access.
- **5. Typography and spacing.**
  - Source Serif only for the page title (32px) and card titles (20px semibold). Inter at 15/1.55 everywhere else.
  - 12px uppercase labels for KPIs only, with a test across `packages/ui` and `apps/web`.
  - `p-6` cards, a 20px Home grid gap, at most 2 weights per row.
  - Slim one-line news and projects cards when they're off.
  - `docs/design/README.md` is updated.
- **Hardening found in review:**
  - `httpsUrl` (https only, no credentials, normalised) for every provider link.
  - One last-`@` `domainOf`.
  - The comms address format is checked against Google's ADDR rule.
  - `name · domain` sender labels on Home and in the Inbox.
  - `cn()` knows the `text-label` size.
