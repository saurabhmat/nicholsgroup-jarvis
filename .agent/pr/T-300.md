## Risks
- Payments are filtered per row by entity; a payment split over two entities would count once per entity (see notes, decision 3).
- The chart is drawn in the browser only (Recharts); not looked at in a browser here.
- The AgedReceivables check compares a whole-company report with the own-entity total; a difference is expected when credits exist or when the lane splits entities (the page says so).
- The nightly reports job may leave the cached aging report one day older than the data: the check line then says "older than the latest data" instead of a difference.
