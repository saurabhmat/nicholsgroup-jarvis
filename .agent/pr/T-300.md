# T-300 + T-301: Finance tabs, Aged AR by customer, invoices and received payments by week

Spec: docs/specs/finance.md 15.1 to 15.3. One branch, one PR. T-302 (cash) is not in it; no Cash tab.

## Changes
- **SQL** `supabase/core/supabase/migrations/20261206100000_finance_reports_ar_weekly.sql` (no new table, no RLS change):
  - `core.finance_receivables(p_lane)`: aged AR by customer; the open set of `core.home_finance_detail`; buckets, 50 customers x 25 invoices, check line.
  - `core.finance_weekly(p_lane, p_weeks default null)`: current week to date + N completed weeks (Sat-Fri New York): invoices (count, sum of total), payments (count of payments, sum of all their rows).
  - `core.finance_week_detail(p_lane, p_week, p_weeks default null)`: invoices and payments of one chosen week (50 each).
  - `core.finance_report_weeks(p_lane)`: the one SECURITY DEFINER; one integer from lane_config `finance.reports.weeks` for a finance reader.
  - The first three: SECURITY INVOKER, stable, `search_path = ''`, read-only, same gate as `home_finance_detail` (finance view + AAL2, current connections only).
- **lane_config**: `finance.reports.weeks` (integer 1 to 13, default 4) in `packages/core/src/lane-config/index.ts`.
- **Web**: `apps/web/lib/finance-reports.ts` (mappers, loaders, tabs, wording), `components/finance-report-frame.tsx` (tab row, states),
  `finance-receivables.tsx`, `finance-weekly.tsx`, `finance-weekly-chart.tsx` (Recharts, client), routes `app/[laneId]/finance/{receivables,weekly}/{page,loading,error}.tsx`,
  tab row on the Overview. `home-finance.ts`: two helpers extracted (`emptyTextFor`, `connectionNoteFor`), behaviour unchanged.
- **Dependency**: `recharts` 3.11.1 (exact) in `apps/web`; it was in the stack but not installed.
- **Generated**: `packages/core/src/db/types.ts` (four functions).
- **Production SQL (gitignored, on disk)**: `part-38-T-300.sql`, `part-38-T-300-check.sql`, `pkg-finance-reports-preview.sql` in `supabase/core/bootstrap/manual-migrations/`.

## Risks
- Payments are filtered per row by entity; a payment split over two entities would count once per entity (see notes, decision 3).
- The chart is drawn in the browser only (Recharts); not looked at in a browser here.
- The AgedReceivables check compares a whole-company report with the own-entity total; a difference is expected when credits exist or when the lane splits entities (the page says so).
- The nightly reports job may leave the cached aging report one day older than the data: the check line then says "older than the latest data" instead of a difference.
