## Risks and notes
- Default Day on phones is chosen from the User-Agent (no script); a desktop browser with a phone UA gets Day. The URL always wins.
- Hour grid positions use wall-clock minutes, so on Nov 1 the repeated 1 AM shows on one row and on Mar 8 the 2 AM row is empty.
- Popovers are `<details>` (no Escape key to close; click the event again).
- The 501-row signal differs slightly from "500 rows"; documented in the migration.
