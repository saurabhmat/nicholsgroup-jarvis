# T-242: QuickBooks normalizer SQLSTATE 21000: root cause pg-safeupdate; step in errors; no unfiltered UPDATE/DELETE

## Root cause (found by Saurabh, Nov 13)
- PostgREST connects as `authenticator`, whose role settings preload `supautils, safeupdate`. This is Supabase's default, in production and locally.
- pg-safeupdate refuses an UPDATE or DELETE without a top-level WHERE with SQLSTATE 21000.
- The normalizer had one: `update pg_temp.q_pay p set ent = coalesce((select ... where ...), ...)`, whose only WHERE was inside a subquery.
- So it succeeded in the SQL Editor (no safeupdate) and failed for the job (through PostgREST).
- Reproduced locally by logging in as `authenticator` and switching to `service_role`:
  - the part 20 body fails with `SQLSTATE 21000, ... step payments`;
  - the fixed body succeeds.

### Fix
- **Migration `20261120110000_fin_qbo_normalize_safeupdate.sql`** (production part 21):
  - The statement gets `where p.id is not null`, which keeps every row.
  - An error with no table or constraint now says "-". Postgres gives an empty string, which made the app's parser drop the step.
- **Every other function** in the app's schemas (reports, home finance, brain, approvals, briefing, assistant, ...) was scanned for UPDATE or DELETE without a top-level WHERE, including dynamic SQL. **None found.**
- **TypeScript** `.update()` / `.delete()` calls through supabase-js were scanned for missing filters. None found; the hits were all Maps, Sets or hashes.
- **Regression:** pgTAP `315_no_unfiltered_update_delete.test.sql` parses every plpgsql/sql function body of the app's schemas. It strips comments and literals, takes each UPDATE or DELETE's own parenthesis level, and requires a WHERE there. It self-tests the production shape, a CTE DELETE, and ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE (allowed).

## Why
In production, PKG's normalizer failed after the first full sync with `fin.qbo_normalize failed: transient: Normalizer unavailable: SQLSTATE 21000`. It was retried for minutes.

## What was found
**The root cause is not identified yet.**
- **(a) Duplicate raw rows: impossible.** `raw.source_records` is unique on (connection, type, id).
- **(b) Several current-settings rows: impossible.** `lane_config_current` is `DISTINCT ON (lane_id)`.
- **No other path found, independently confirmed by the security/code review:**
  - every upsert input is unique by construction;
  - every scalar subquery is aggregated or limited;
  - the triggers and policies have none;
  - production part 4 is byte-identical to the repository.
- **Why the logs can't say more:** the function's handler re-raised every failure with a constant message ("table -"), so where it happened was lost.

## Changes
- **Migration `20261120100000_fin_qbo_normalize_step.sql`** (production part 20) replaces `core.fin_qbo_normalize`; the body is otherwise unchanged:
  - The error names the step: `..., step <word>`, where the word is one of config, effective, accounts, invoices, bills, payments, payment_keys, transactions, transaction_keys or summary. These are fixed words, never data.
  - Before the two upserts whose keys the function builds itself (payment allocations, posting lines), a hash-aggregate check refuses a duplicate key with 21000 under its own step. Nothing is dropped silently, so money rows are never lost.
- **`normalize-store.ts`:**
  - SQLSTATE 21000 is now a permanent `provider_error`: "The normalizer found two rows for one record (SQLSTATE 21000, step ...)". It is no longer retried.
  - Refusal messages keep the step.

## Review
The first version deduped every upsert input with self-join deletes. The review found that this was quadratic (33.5 s for 20k posting lines, against the 100 s timeout) and that it could never fire. It also found that silently dropping money rows breaks rule 7. That version was replaced by the checks above, which take about 12 ms on 20k rows.
