## What it does
- **Reply drafts on Home:** up to 3 per briefing, from the T-231c run (`apps/web/components/home-reply-drafts.tsx`). Each draft shows:
  - the thread (subject masked, rule 7), the recipient, the mailbox, the received time and an "Open thread" link;
  - its status: **Ready for your review** or **Awaiting your input**;
  - the context note and the copy-ready text with a **Copy** button (`copy-text-button.tsx`);
  - **Review in Approvals**, linking to the card (`#action-<id>`).
- **Other states on Home:**
  - A draft that needs the owner shows "No text yet" and is never proposed.
  - Further threads needing a reply are listed by sender name.
  - On a re-run, Home links to "An earlier draft is waiting in Approvals".
  - When proposing failed, it says "Could not be added to Approvals: copy it instead" (never a raw code).
  - If the lane does not store outbound drafts (D-19), it says so.
- **Proposals:** every **ready** draft becomes an `email.send` proposal (`apps/web/lib/briefing-run.ts` `proposeReplies`, via `@nichols/approvals/wire` `proposeBriefingReply`):
  - Recipient: the sender of the message replied to, from mail metadata. Never the lane's own mailboxes, never a no-reply address.
  - Subject: the real subject with "Re:" (keeps Gmail threading; Home shows it masked).
  - Thread and message replied to: from metadata. The sending mailbox: the connection's.
  - From the model: only the body text and the context note (shown as "not verified").
  - The proposal is `proposerKind: "job"` and `derivedFromRetrieved: true`, so the card shows the banner and the recipient flags.
  - Proposals are created before the briefing is saved, so the saved draft carries its proposal id.
- **Nothing is sent.** Proposals only; the owner edits and approves in Approvals (T-232).
- **Never proposed:**
  - a draft with a masked figure (`[amount]`, `[number]`);
  - an ungrounded draft (its text is emptied);
  - a draft with a link or email address that is not in its own thread (rule 9: another email in the same model call may have planted it; note `foreign_link:<ref>.reply`).
- **SQL** (`act.insert_proposal`, in the T-232 migration; not in production yet), under a per-lane advisory lock:
  - A job may have at most 6 pending proposals, so the owner keeps room.
  - At most one job reply per message replied to: none while one is pending, approved, being sent or sent; none for 7 days after a rejection.
- **Errors:** a proposal failure never stops the briefing; it leaves a code in the notes.
