## 1. Cash (book balance) from the current balances of PKG's mapped bank accounts
- **Migration `20261121100000_cash_from_bank_balances.sql`** (production part 22):
  - `fin.accounts.current_balance numeric(18,2)`, nullable (QuickBooks `Account.CurrentBalance`).
  - `core.fin_qbo_normalize` sets it on insert and on update (it is in the "is distinct from" tuple). Based on the part 21 body; only the accounts step changed. The `where p.id is not null` safeupdate predicate and all step markers are kept.
  - `core.home_finance` no longer reads the balance sheet. Cash = sum of `current_balance` of active, not deleted bank accounts that are listed in `entities.bank_accounts` with owner = own entity (D-18). An account under another owner, under more than one owner, or whose entity_code is another entity is flagged `shared`. A bank account in no mapping is counted in `cash_unmapped` and not summed. `cash_as_of` = last books sync (`connections.last_sync_at`).
  - Reasons: `bank_mapping_missing`, `no_own_bank_accounts`, `balance_unreadable` (an own account has no balance yet; the text says a re-process fills it). The balance-sheet reasons are gone.
  - Label stays "book balance"; output column names unchanged. No new table, so no new RLS surface.
- TS: reason texts in `apps/web/lib/home-finance.ts`, generated DB types.
- **Tests:** pgTAP 300 (rewritten cash section: own mapped only, other owner / unmapped / inactive / deleted / non-bank left out, shared flags, as_of = last sync, unreadable balance, no own account, class-filtered balance sheet has no effect, cross-lane and AAL1 denial kept), new 316 (column, stored on insert, rounded, null when absent or not a number, updated on change, identical re-run changes nothing, through `service_role`), 130 updated for the one new money column. 315 (no unfiltered UPDATE/DELETE) passes.
