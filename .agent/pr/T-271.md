## Changes
- **Side menu, the same for every company** (`packages/ui/src/lib/nav.ts`, `SIDE_MENU`): Home, Inbox, Calendar, Claude, ChatGPT, Finance, Tasks, Contacts, Messages, Settings.
- **Tasks** is the Projects page renamed. Its URL stays `/<company>/projects`. The Home card now says "Tasks at a glance".
- **Claude and ChatGPT** (`/<company>/claude`, `/<company>/chatgpt`): each page has one button that opens the vendor's own site in a new tab (`rel="noopener noreferrer"`).
  - The link is a fixed address with no query, so nothing of the portal is sent.
  - Each person signs in there with their own account.
  - It is a link, not an embed: both sites normally refuse to load inside another site's page. See Risks.
- **Contacts and Messages**: placeholder pages. Messages says it is for Microsoft Teams, once connected.
- **Get started / AI brain, Approvals and Assistant** have no menu entry any more. Their pages are unchanged.
  - Settings has a new first section, "Approvals and Assistant", with the two links. A company that hides one of those modules shows only the other.
  - Get started is the existing "AI brain" section of Settings ("Continue setup").
  - While one of the three pages is open, Settings is the selected menu entry.
- **iPhone bottom tabs**: Home, Inbox, Calendar, Claude, More. More lists ChatGPT, Finance, Tasks, Contacts, Messages and Settings. The "Get started" row on More is gone.
- **Removed from the shell**: the setup badge ("3 of 10") and the approvals count. The count was never passed by the app, so no badge showed before either. The layout no longer reads setup progress on every page.
- **Module hiding (T-265) is unchanged**: a company whose settings limit its modules, such as a mail-only company, still hides Finance and Tasks. The four new pages belong to no module, so every company shows them.
- No SQL, no new environment variable, no company-specific code.

## Risks
- **Embedding.** Saurabh asked for Claude and ChatGPT to be embedded, possibly with a third-party tool. Both sites normally send headers that forbid loading them inside another site's frame, and a browser enforces that whatever tool is used. This was not checked against the live sites from here. The pages therefore open the site in a new tab. If an embed method is found, it replaces the card on the same page.
- **Approvals are one step further away** (Settings, then Approvals). Drafts still show where they did on Home and in the Inbox. The final place for Approvals and Assistant is undecided (the idea is a sub-section under Inbox).
- **The phone's "Ask" tab is gone**; the Assistant is reached from Settings. "Ask Jarvis" on Home still links to it.
