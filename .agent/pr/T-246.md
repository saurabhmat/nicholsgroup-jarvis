# T-246: Home and Finance read only the current QuickBooks connection

## Why
At go-live (H-061) the sandbox QuickBooks connection is disconnected, but its rows stay. Cash, Net income and the Reports list already read only the lane's current connections (status not `disconnected`, from `core.home_finance_context.qbo_connections`).

These still read `fin.invoices`, `fin.bills` and `fin.report_cache` by lane only:
- AR over 30 days;
- AR aging and the three oldest invoices;
- bills due in 14 days;
- the unmapped invoice and bill counts;
- the Finance AR total;
- the "has data" test.

So the sandbox's invoices (AR over 30 today: $5,281.52) would have been added to the real company's figures.

## Risks
- **Previously connected companies:** a lane that intentionally uses two live QuickBooks connections at once would have both summed, as before. That's not a PKG case: golive-1 refuses to pin the realm while another company's connection is live.
- **Disconnected connections in general:** their rows are kept but ignored. If a connection is disconnected by mistake, Home shows "not connected" (no live connection) rather than stale figures.
