# T-244: Finance page and Net income from live QuickBooks data

## Why Home's Net income said "not synced yet"
`core.home_finance` reads the cached P&L whose `start_date` is the first day of this month (New York). The reports job only fetched the configured period (year to date, start Jan 1, or trailing 12 months), so outside January no row matched. A cache-key mismatch, not a time zone or fiscal-year issue.

## Changes
- `packages/connectors/src/quickbooks/reports.ts`: `planReports` also requests the P&L for this business week (Sat to today), month to date and calendar year to date (deduplicated, same method/class rules, no new params key). New `businessWeekStart`.
- SQL `20261122100000_finance_page.sql`: `core.finance_pnl_window` (shared P&L extraction), `core.home_finance` now uses it (same output), `core.finance_pnl(lane, period)`, `core.finance_overview(lane)`. All SECURITY INVOKER, empty search_path, revoked from public/anon, granted to authenticated and service_role. No table.
- Finance page (`apps/web/components/finance-page.tsx`, `apps/web/lib/finance-page.ts`): every card wired to SQL. Cash (book balance), Accounts receivable (equals the aging total), Bills due in 14 days, Net income for week/month/year (`?period=`), Receivables aging, Cash by account (balance, shared flag, last sync), Controls (what exists, plus a line for what is missing), Reports (cached report metadata). Subscriptions unchanged.
- db types regenerated; pgTAP catalog allowlist updated.

## Risks
- Up to 3 more QuickBooks calls per nightly run per connection (rate limit 450/min; fine). More report_cache rows per night (retention job still open).
- Week and month figures are as old as the last nightly run; the card shows the report's own as-of and range. On a new month/week the figure reads "not synced yet" until the next run.
- Calendar year, not fiscal (no fiscal start in lane_config).
- A pgTAP test (317) inserts a week row and a month row with the same as_of; on a day when week and month windows are identical (a Saturday that is the 1st) it would hit a unique key. Rare.
