# T-040: conn.token_health and subscriptions_renew skeleton

## Summary
Two scheduled scans and two per-connection workers in Inngest.

**Daily token health check** (`conn-token-health-scan`, 11:00 UTC). It sends one `conn/health.check.requested` per usable connection in every enabled lane. The worker `conn-token-health`:
- flags a credential (`token_expires_at`) that expires within 7 days or has already expired;
- runs the provider's read-only health check, which today exists for QuickBooks only (CompanyInfo);
- on a failed check, writes the redacted error to `core.connections.last_error` and moves `active` to `degraded`;
- on a good check, moves `degraded` back to `active` and clears only the health check's own error;
- emits `conn/attention.needed` for each issue.

**Twice-daily renewal scan** (`conn-subscriptions-renew-scan`) and the worker `conn-subscriptions-renew` define the renewal interface (`SubscriptionRenewer`). The registry is empty, so no external call or write happens today.

No SQL change: `core.connections` already has the columns.

## Changes
- `packages/core/src/inngest/conn-health.ts` (new):
  - the four functions;
  - workers keyed by `laneId:connectionId` with a limit of 1 and a rate limit of 1 per hour, and 2 retries;
  - scans are not retried;
  - the check and the renewal run inside `step.run`;
  - events are validated again, and lanes not in `ENABLED_LANES` are skipped;
  - alerts are schema-checked before they are sent, with lane-scoped ids, one per day.
- `events.ts`: `conn/health.check.requested`, `conn/subscription.renew.requested` and `conn/attention.needed`. All require `laneId` and carry ids and enums only; there is no message text.
- `index.ts`: exports. `apps/web/app/api/inngest/route.ts`: registers the four functions.
- `packages/connectors/src/health/` (new):
  - `job.ts`: the expiry rule, the health and renewal jobs, and the `HEALTH_CHECK_ENDPOINT` marker;
  - `store.ts`: lane-scoped listing; updates guarded on the loaded status; `clearHealthError`;
  - `types.ts`, `index.ts` (QuickBooks checker; empty renewer registry).
- `vault/index.ts` builds the store from the lane job client. `package.json` adds the `./health` export.
