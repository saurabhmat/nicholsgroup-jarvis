# T-051: QuickBooks entity sync (CDC)

## Summary
This adds the QuickBooks runner for `conn.sync`:
- A full initial load, and after that Change Data Capture (CDC), for 11 entities: Account, Customer, Vendor, Invoice, Payment, Bill, BillPayment, Purchase, Deposit, JournalEntry and Class.
- It reads the company Preferences and keeps the connection's feature flags up to date.
- Records go through a strict allowlist into `raw.source_records`, stored only for the lane that owns the connection.

There is no SQL change:
- The cursor uses the `default` stream of `core.sync_cursor_put` (compare-and-swap).
- The flags live in the existing `core.connections.features`.
- The audit goes through `audit.log`.

## Changes
- **`packages/connectors/src/quickbooks/sync.ts`** (new):
  - full load with paged `select ... ORDERBY Id STARTPOSITION n MAXRESULTS 1000`;
  - CDC with a look-back of at most 30 days minus 1 hour;
  - deletions as tombstones;
  - a targeted (singleton) run;
  - 450/min rate limit, with backoff on 429.
- **`quickbooks/entities.ts`** (new): a Zod allowlist projection for each entity.
  - **Payroll is excluded (D-25, default (b)):**
    - Payroll accounts are found from configured ids, Intuit payroll subtypes, a name safety net and sub-accounts.
    - A transaction is excluded whole if any account ref points at a payroll account, it names an Employee or another person who isn't a customer or vendor, or it names an excluded entity id.
    - Excluded objects become Id-only tombstones.
  - **Masking:** digit runs of 5+ in names and 8+ in DocNumbers (Unicode-aware).
- **`quickbooks/features.ts`** (new): reads Preferences (class tracking, departments, multi-currency) into `core.connections.features`, audited through `withAudit`.
- **`packages/core/src/lane-config`:** a new `finance.payroll { account_ids, account_subtypes, excluded_entity_ids }` section.
  - It has defaults.
  - Its Zod schema rejects unknown keys and malformed lists.
  - If the setting can't be read, the sync stops before any call and the cursor doesn't move.
- **Wiring:** `vault/index.ts` (`qboFeatureStore`), `vault/lane-config.ts` (`payrollSettings`), and the quickbooks runner in `sync/index.ts`.
- **Test support:** a synthetic MSW fake of Intuit (`testing/msw/quickbooks/fixtures.ts` and `handlers.ts`) and the harness.
- **Docs:**
  - `docs/backlog.md`: H-034 (sandbox live check), the D-25 question for Robert, and the T-056 acceptance addition;
  - `docs/plan-review.md`: the review log.

## Tests

## Risks
- **Untested against Intuit:** everything was tested against a synthetic fake only. The query syntax, the CDC shape, the payroll subtype spellings and the Preferences field names are unverified (H-034).
- **Wages on an ordinary account:** wages booked to a non-payroll account aren't recognised. Configure `account_ids` for PKG if needed.
- **Names:** customer and vendor `DisplayName`s are stored and can be an individual's name.
