## Ranking (part 1)
- Config: `lane_config.briefing.attention.fresh_hours` (int 6..168, default 48) and `briefing.deadline_subjects`. Also `briefing.exclusions.own_domain_bulk_senders` (part 3c). Nothing company-specific in code.
- Not a blended score: importance band first, then NEWEST first. The window does the "recency" work (a thread older than the window is not ranked at all), so a 2 h unread always beats a 6-day read-not-answered thread, and a 40 h VIP still beats a 1 h unread (band wins inside the window). Boundary: exactly `fresh_hours` old is fresh, a minute older is Still waiting.
- Bands high to low: VIP (authenticated priority sender); Money or Deadline (a waiting/unread thread from a person whose subject matches the money patterns or `deadline_subjects`); waiting; unread person; security; repeated failures.
- Money/Deadline band applies only to threads that qualify anyway (so automated billing mail stays on the Money data, not the top 5).
- Still waiting = qualifying threads older than the window of kind priority / waiting / unread. No exception for priority senders (as the brief said). Old security alerts and old repeated-failure groups are dropped (not counted): they do not wait on a reply. `more` counts only fresh items beyond 5.
- Still waiting line: "Still waiting: N older threads" (singular for 1), hidden at 0, shown even when the top 5 is empty. Link: Inbox `?view=waiting`. No existing Inbox filter fit (unread/important are SQL filters and old read threads are not "unread"), and a SQL filter was not worth a migration. `?view=waiting` is computed in TS from the same `rankAttention` (`waitingFor` in `lib/inbox.ts`), metadata only, via the caller's own lane client (RLS). Capped by `briefing_threads` (300 threads, `attention_days` 7 by default).
- AI input: the model gets the fresh list; the older count as a number only ("Older threads still waiting (not listed, number only): N"). Money refs older than the window are left out of the refs too (they stay in the Money data and the Still waiting count). All times in the facts are Eastern.

## Waiting on you (part 2)

- Chip rule (`waitingOnYou`): `lastToOwner && !lastIsOwner`. Applies to any kind from a person (waiting or unread), not only kind "waiting". Chips, at most 2, in order: VIP, Waiting on you, Money, Deadline, Unread, Security alert, Repeated failures. The priority chip label is now "VIP" (was "Priority sender").
- Reason texts: "Waiting on your reply" only when the owner is in the To; otherwise "Read, no reply from you" / "New message after your reply". "Read, not answered" stays for read mail in the To.
- Deadline default list (generic, whole words): deadline, due today, due tomorrow, by EOD, RFP, proposal due, expires.
